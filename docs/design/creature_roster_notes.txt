Types
There are 12 types, arranged in four rings of three. Inside a ring, each type beats the next one and loses to the one before it, like rock-paper-scissors. Against types outside its ring, a type is neutral.

Temperaments
Every creature is either a Diva or Shy, 20 of each. Temperament is a personality, separate from type. It could also drive a small battle rule:
Diva: hits harder on the turn after landing a hit, because it's feeling itself. Loses some accuracy after taking a big hit, because it's sulking.
Shy: dodges more easily on its first turn out, since it's still hiding. Hits softer while the opponent is at full health, because it hasn't worked up the nerve yet.
Each creature has three attacks. The third, marked (signature), is its own and says something about its personality. Creatures with finished sprites are marked sprites done.
