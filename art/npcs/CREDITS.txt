NN_name.svg  (15 sheets, plus README.txt from the original pack)
  NPC walk sheets: each 48x64 pixels = 3x4 frames of 16x16, transparent background.
  Rows: down, up, left, right. Columns: standing, step, other step (same layout as the player).
  Original pixel art made for Procedural Adventures, supplied by The Buffalo, New York Procedural Amusements Coding Society (October 2026)
  as rpg_sprite_sheets.zip. That pack also had a PNG of every sheet and a combined atlas; the PNGs
  matched these SVGs pixel for pixel when imported, so only the SVGs are kept as the source.

  Converted to src/gen/people.js by tools/build_people.py (the files themselves are unchanged).
  Which game role uses which sheet: the PA.SPR string mapping in src/game/maps.js.
