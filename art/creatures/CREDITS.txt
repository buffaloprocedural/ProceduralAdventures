Original creature battle sets - in the game

Eleven creatures supplied by The Buffalo, New York Procedural Amusements Coding Society (October 2026) in creature_battle_sets.zip, unchanged:
  glumph (Water), hexkit (Shadow), hoarfowl (Ice), ironpurr (Metal), lorislurk (Toxic), lumikit (Light),
  pinkadillo (Light), potoom (Shadow), prismmer (Light), riptabby (Water), servolt (Storm)
Each SVG is 80x32: front view 32x32 at (0,0), back view 32x32 at (32,0), and two 16x16 party-menu icon
frames at (64,0) and (64,16) - the same layout as art/mascot/gulliver_battle.svg (GULLET, from the same
pack, is in the game as GULLIVER's species). tools/sprite_sheet.py reads them as they are.

They are part of the game's creature roster (src/game/roster.js, from docs/design/); the elemental names above are
the pack's, and the game's types are HOARFOWL FROST, RIPTABBY and GLUMPH
TIDE, SERVOLT CURRENT, IRONPURR CHROME, POTOOM and HEXKIT HUSH, PRISMMER, LUMIKIT and PINKADILLO GLAM, LORISLURK
BLIGHT. tools/build_creatures.py builds them into src/gen/creatures.js. SERVOLT and GLUMPH were later recoloured (steel blue,
sea green) by tools/recolour_creatures.py. The roster's other creatures were drawn with vectors
(tools/creature_rig.py; options in options/ and ideas/, chosen by the Society). A new creature gets a plain
placeholder shape until a finished set is saved here as <name>.svg (or .png), lower-case, same 80x32 layout.
