hero_masc_1..6.svg, hero_femme_1..6.svg
  The protagonist's twelve looks: walk sheets of 48x64 pixels = 3x4 frames of 16x16.
  Rows: down, up, left, right. Columns: standing, step, other step.
  Original pixel art made for Procedural Adventures, supplied by The Buffalo, New York Procedural Amusements Coding Society (October 2026) in
  hero_variants.zip, unchanged; VARIANTS_README.txt (their README) describes each look. hero_masc_1 is the
  original hero.svg, which it replaces. The .png/.gif files here are the Society's previews.

  After choosing BOY or GIRL the player is given one of that kind's six looks at random, never the same look as
  the previous new game in that browser (PA.pickLook in src/main.js). Converted to src/gen/hero.js by
  tools/build_hero.py.

  The original hero.svg carried an embedded C2PA provenance manifest recording that it was produced with
  Claude (Anthropic) at the Society's request.
