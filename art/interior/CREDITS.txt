Interior tileset - original pixel art made for Procedural Adventures, supplied by The Buffalo, New York Procedural Amusements Coding Society
(October 2026) as files2.zip:

  interior_tileset.svg            the tileset (256x192 = 16x12 tiles of 16x16). Source of record. Floors, walls and
                                  stairs are opaque "base" tiles; furniture and wall decor have transparent backgrounds
                                  and are drawn over the base as an "object" layer. Its embedded C2PA manifest (left
                                  intact) records that it was produced with Claude (Anthropic).
  interior_tileset_manifest.json  the name, position, size and layer of every tile and multi-tile piece
  interior_tileset_preview_3x.png the same tileset at 3x on a grey background (it matches the SVG pixel for pixel
                                  wherever the SVG is opaque)
  interior_demo_rooms.png         reference rooms showing how the pieces go together

All four files are unchanged. tools/build_interior_tiles.py reads the SVG into src/gen/interior_tiles.js; the
game's furnishing pass (src/game/furnish.js) lays every room out in it.
