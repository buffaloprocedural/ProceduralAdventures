gulliver_overworld.svg
  Walk sheet: 48x64 = 3x4 frames of 16x16. Rows down, up, left, right; columns stand, step, other step.

gulliver_battle.svg
  His complete battle set, 80x32: front view 32x32 at (0,0), back view 32x32 at (32,0), and two 16x16
  party-menu icon frames at (64,0) and (64,16). Supplied by The Buffalo, New York Procedural Amusements Coding Society (October 2026) as gullet.svg
  in creature_battle_sets.zip; unchanged. It replaces the earlier gulliver_battle_8x.png (a front view
  only, which the game mirrored for his back).

Both are original pixel art made for Procedural Adventures. tools/build_mascot.py turns them into
src/gen/mascot.js.
